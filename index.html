About

Samuel Kwame Oteng is a public administrator whose career in Ghana's Civil Service spans more than a decade. He rose from Assistant Director to Deputy Director at the Ministry of Fisheries and Aquaculture, working on policy planning, performance reporting and Cabinet submissions.

He contributed to drafting the Fisheries and Aquaculture Act, 2025, served as national focal person for the FAO Port State Measures Agreement, and helped coordinate Ghana's dialogue with the European Union on illegal, unreported and unregulated (IUU) fishing.

He holds a Master of Public Sector Management from GIMPA and a First Class BA in Political Science with Archaeology from the University of Ghana. His papers cover democracy, globalisation and electoral politics in Sub-Saharan Africa.
